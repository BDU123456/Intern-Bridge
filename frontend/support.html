Support

We’re here to help you use Hintern Bridge effectively.

Contact Support

Email: support@internbridge.com

Hours: Monday–Friday, 9:00–17:00 (UTC)

Common Support Topics

  • Account access issues and password resets
  • Posting or editing internship listings
  • Application submissions and attachments
  • Profile updates and privacy settings

Response Times

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 1–2 business days. Complex issues may take longer.

Reporting Issues

If you encounter a bug or security concern, please include detailed steps to reproduce and any relevant screenshots. For security issues, use the subject line: SECURITY.

About Hintern Bridge

Hintern Bridge connects technology students with companies, helping students discover internships, build portfolios, and launch their careers while enabling companies to find emerging talent.
