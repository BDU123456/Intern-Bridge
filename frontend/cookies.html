Cookie Policy

Learn what cookies we use and how you can control them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They help websites remember information about your visit.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Strictly Necessary: essential for core functionality (e.g., login).
  • Performance: help us understand how the site is used to improve it.
  • Functionality: remember your preferences and settings.
  • Analytics: measure engagement and performance.

3. Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling some cookies may affect site functionality.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services used by Hintern Bridge may set their own cookies, subject to their policies.

5. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact: privacy@internbridge.com.
