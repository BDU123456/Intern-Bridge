Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account data such as name, email, profile details.
  • Usage data such as pages visited, interactions, device and browser type.
  • Content you submit including resumes, listings, and messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and improve our services.
  • Match students with relevant internships and companies with candidates.
  • Communicate updates, security alerts, and support responses.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with service providers under confidentiality agreements, and with companies/students according to platform functionality, or when required by law.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services and comply with legal obligations. You can request deletion subject to legal and operational constraints.

5. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  • Object to or restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

Hintern Bridge is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will post the updated policy with the effective date.

9. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, contact: privacy@internbridge.com.
